Privacy Policy for CommitFit

Effective Date: February 4, 2026
Last Updated: February 4, 2026

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect, use, and share your information. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for the CommitFit iOS application and this website.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • App Settings: Goals, workout-type preferences, custom colors, filters, and notification preferences
  • Notes: Optional notes you add to goals
  • Support Requests: Information you share if you contact us by email

B. Health Data (via Apple Health)

  • Workout Data: Workout type, start/end time, duration, distance, and calories burned
  • Read-Only Access: We only read workout data and do not write to HealthKit

C. Website Data

  • Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized usage data via Google Analytics 4 (cookieless configuration)

2. How We Use Your Information

A. Core App Functionality

  • Provide and maintain workout goals, heatmap, analytics, and widgets
  • Calculate progress, trends, and personal records locally on your device
  • Store your preferences and settings on your device

B. Analytics and Improvement

  • Improve the website experience using aggregated analytics
  • Fix bugs and optimize app performance

3. Information Sharing

We share your information with the following third parties:

A. Service Providers

  • Google Analytics 4: Aggregated website analytics (cookieless configuration)
  • Apple HealthKit: Workout data access (read-only) for app features
  • Apple StoreKit/App Store: In-app purchase processing for premium upgrades

B. Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

C. Business Transfers

In case of merger, acquisition, or sale, user information may be transferred as part of business assets.

4. Data Retention

  • Health and goal data: Stored locally on your device until you delete the app or reset your data
  • Website analytics: Retained according to analytics provider settings

5. Children's Privacy

CommitFit is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

6. International Users

A. Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where our service providers operate.

B. European Union Users

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with:

  • Apple Health
    Purpose: Read workout data for goals, analytics, and heatmaps
    Data Shared: Workout data (read-only)
    Privacy Policy
  • Apple StoreKit / App Store
    Purpose: In-app purchase processing for premium upgrades
    Data Shared: Transaction data handled by Apple
    Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics 4 (Website)
    Purpose: Website analytics (cookieless configuration)
    Data Shared: Aggregated, anonymized usage data
    Privacy Policy

9. Your Rights and Choices

A. Opt-Out Options

  • Health Access: Manage HealthKit permissions in the Health app
  • Notifications: Disable in iOS Settings > Notifications

B. Data Requests

Contact us to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete local app data (you can also delete the app)
  • Export your data (CSV export available to premium users)

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of material changes through:

  • App update notifications
  • In-app notifications

Continued use of our app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices:
Email: artur.uff@gmail.com
For GDPR-related requests, please include "GDPR Request" in your subject line.

12. App Store Compliance

This privacy policy complies with:

  • Apple App Store Review Guidelines
  • GDPR and CCPA privacy regulations
