About

I'm a Cloud Platform Engineer at Centene, where I build AWS infrastructure for healthcare data operations serving millions of members — Lambda, ECS, S3, SQS, DynamoDB, Step Functions and Glue, provisioned with Terraform through CI/CD, and kept alive through on-call incident response.

Before engineering, I spent seven years as a criminal defense attorney in Ukraine. Reading a statute closely and reading a stack trace closely turn out to be the same skill, and the switch is the most useful thing about how I work: I'm comfortable in the ambiguous, high-stakes middle of a problem where the answer isn't documented yet.

The rest of my time goes to things I build on my own: agent tooling on the Claude and OpenAI APIs, AWS Bedrock and the MCP protocol, a set of iOS apps, and lately some web projects. AI and distributed systems are what I actually find interesting — the day job and the night work point the same direction.
